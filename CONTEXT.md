# Portfolio

The Owner's CV presented as an interactive site, made for hiring managers and recruiters.
A Visitor arrives on the Landing, opens the Board, and explores the Owner through its Panels.

## Language

### People

**Owner**:
The person the site is about and who maintains it.
_Avoid_: admin, author, me

**Visitor**:
Someone browsing the site, typically a hiring manager or recruiter.
_Avoid_: user, recruiter, client

### Screens

**Landing**:
The first screen, showing the animated logo and whether the backend is reachable. Clicking the logo reveals the Board.
_Avoid_: home, intro, splash

**Board**:
The screen that holds the five Panels. It is the whole site after the Landing.
_Avoid_: menu, overlay, hub

**Panel**:
One of the Board's five areas, in order: About, What I do, Skills, Chat with me, Contact.
_Avoid_: block, column, section, tile

**Expanded panel**:
The one Panel opened to fill the Board and show its content. At most one at a time.
_Avoid_: active block, open section

**Stripe**:
A Panel squeezed to the Board's edge while another Panel is expanded, showing only its title.
_Avoid_: collapsed column, sidebar

**Sublabel**:
An optional short line under a Panel's title, shown only while no Panel is expanded.
_Avoid_: subtitle, tagline

### Content

**Skill**:
Something the Owner knows, explained in their own words, with no proficiency rating.
_Avoid_: competency, rating

**Certificate**:
A credential the Owner holds, listed with the Skills.
_Avoid_: certification, badge

**Takeover**:
One of exactly 36 kinds of work the Owner can take over: what it is, why the Owner fits, and what
the Owner does in that regard.
_Avoid_: service, offering, skill

**Takeover group**:
One of six groups of six Takeovers. Group and position map onto a throw of two dice.
_Avoid_: category

**CV**:
The Owner's downloadable résumé document, in English and German.
_Avoid_: résumé, profile

### Contact

**Contact request**:
What a Visitor leaves for the Owner: an optional name, a company, an optional short position
description and a way to reach them.
_Avoid_: lead, message, inquiry

**Legal notice**:
The Owner's name and postal address that German law requires the site to show.
_Avoid_: Impressum, imprint

**Privacy policy**:
The statement of which personal data the site collects, why, and for how long.
_Avoid_: Datenschutz, data policy

### Chat

**Agent**:
The AI counterpart a Visitor talks to in the Chat with me Panel, answering about the Owner.
_Avoid_: bot, assistant, avatar

**Knowledge**:
Everything the Agent knows about the Owner: Panel content, Vault notes and Interview answers.
_Avoid_: training data, knowledge base, memory

**Memory**:
What the Agent retains about a Visitor across conversations, including those conversations.
_Avoid_: history, context, Knowledge

**Vault**:
The Owner's Obsidian notes on technical knowledge: approaches, books, videos.
_Avoid_: notes, wiki

**Interview**:
A long text Q&A in which an AI interviewer questions the Owner to build the Knowledge.
_Avoid_: questionnaire, Q&A session

# Headline questionnaire

The Headline is the one line after "Hello, I'm Alexander Durnev. I'm a {Headline}." It names what the Owner is professionally and points at the target roles.

**CV seed**: "Senior Full Stack Engineer | Technical Lead", 20 years of experience, TypeScript/JavaScript expert, practical Rust/Python/PHP, AI integration.

## Round 1

1. Which target roles should a Visitor read in it? (Senior Full Stack Engineer, Tech Lead, Staff/Principal, Engineering partner, AI engineer…) Rank them.
2. Frontend-first, backend-first or truly full stack? Which one do you want to be hired as?
3. Should the Headline mention TypeScript, AI, or neither?
4. Years of experience in the Headline, or only in the Bio?
5. Tone: plain title ("Senior Full Stack Engineer"), or a title plus a hook ("…who ships AI features")?

### Answers

- Target: Senior Full-Stack Engineer combined with System Design Specialist.
- Hireable as backend, frontend or full stack; good results across all three.
- Plain title, no technology or AI in it. Years of experience only in the Bio.

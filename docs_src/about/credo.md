# Credo questionnaire

The credo is one line under the greeting, a little bigger than the sample's quote. It combines experience, passion for technology, team spirit and love of experimentation. It is not the Bio.

**CV seed**: "collaborative team-work mindset, engineering excellence", mentoring, "20 years", agile (scrum or kanban), distributed international teams.

## Round 1

1. In your words: what do you believe about building software after 20 years?
2. Which one image or phrase says "passion for technology" for you, not a cliché?
3. What does team spirit mean in practice for you (mentoring, reviews, unblocking others)?
4. An experiment that went somewhere: one example the line could hint at.
5. Voice: calm and confident, warm, or playful?

### Answers

- Belief: a profession of constant change; to stay an engineer you learn new technologies, adapt and experiment. Good design and the fundamentals (R. Martin, K. Beck, J. Ousterhout, M. Fowler, E. Evans and others) are the guardrails, in the AI era too.
- Passion, two parts: (1) the joy of work done properly, a product that works well, meets users' demand and earns endorsing feedback; (2) watching new technology change the industry and the world, opening ideas that could not be considered before.
- Team spirit: well-defined roles and obligations, creativity from collective contribution, mutual respect, success recognised, no toxic attitudes, unity of minds drives progress (mentoring and reviews are part of it).
- Experiment: WebRTC replacing Flash and ActiveX at Zovolt, when the protocol was unstable and not adopted by all major browsers; many experiments to learn what mattered.
- Voice: professional, industry-standard language, confident.

### Draft

Options (one line each, pick one or mix):

1. "Twenty years in a profession of constant change taught me to keep learning, to keep experimenting, and to hold on to fundamentals and to teams that respect each other."
2. "I love software done properly and the new technology that keeps making it possible; solid design keeps the experiments honest, and a team in step makes both worth doing."
3. "Constant change is the job: I learn, I experiment, and I rely on sound design and a respectful team to turn new technology into products people like."


### Approved (round 2)

The Owner picked none of the three options and asked for the earlier phrase instead: "A software engineer who fell for the web 20 years ago and never left. I still set time aside for experiments and discoveries: that's where the better solutions keep coming from." The Bio keeps only its remaining three sentences.
